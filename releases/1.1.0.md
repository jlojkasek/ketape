## Réserve

- **Balance** tracks remaining W′ in percent and kJ, shows whether you are depleting or recovering, and estimates time to empty or to 90% recovery. Larger fields add a live history and minimum reserve.
- **Available Power** estimates the constant power your remaining reserve could support over durations from one to twenty minutes.
- **Insights** summarizes minimum and time-weighted average reserve, work and time above your reference power, capacity equivalents, and time spent in five reserve bands.
- Use your existing Intervals.icu connection to import cycling FTP and W′, or enter critical power and W′ under **Réserve**. Once configured, the fields work offline.
- The three fields adapt to Karoo screen layouts, include animated setup previews, and share the same ride state as you change pages.
- Customize power averaging, graph duration, dynamic colors, and the low-reserve threshold. Time and power estimates assume constant effort and depend on the quality of your power data and parameters.
